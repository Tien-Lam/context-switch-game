# Delivered design and content

Deterministic local simulation with fictional providers/models, quota and context, trust, repository health/debt, evidence-based reviews, structural upgrades and causal incidents. The campaign has 16 main tickets and seven conditional repairs across two chapters. The player supplies judgment; there is no attention/human-bandwidth resource. Both providers start installed; execution capacity grows from one to three slots. Intended work passes remain short; richer provider voices and wider audience validation are future product research, not unfinished alpha acceptance.
