# Sidebar browser gameplay evidence

The primary coordinator played the local baseline in the visible in-app browser on 5 October 2026 Sydney time. This report captures the first five shipments and concrete interaction weaknesses for the improvement backlog. The coordinator already knew the product and implementation; this is an expert browser run, not an independent novice test or a blind comparison.

## Build and coverage

Baseline commit: e1c55d8. URL: http://127.0.0.1:5173/. Fresh local run in an agent-created sidebar tab. Tools: actual in-app browser accessibility controls, typed commands, tab clicks and visible-state checks. No injected save, game state or source-driven browser mutation.

Policy: fast shipper with a deliberate unsafe privacy approval to test recovery, followed by a safe reduced-scope cache fix. Coverage: two launched providers, parallel work, cross-provider review, five shipments, a repair and a generic revision. Twenty-two typed commands and three in-game tab switches were counted in the route below. Simulated wait and active-play timing were not instrumented; browser-tool time must not be reported as human pacing. The displayed initial work estimates were 4 seconds for APP-101, 7 for APP-118, 6 for PERF-204, 6 for FIX-118 and 6 for PLAT-77. No deliberate blocking sleep was used.

In Terminal 1: `anthill`; `Please take the next ticket`; `What changed in APP-101?`; `What reviews need a decision?`; `Approve APP-101`; `Please take the next ticket`.

Back in Terminal 2: `Take PLAT-77`; `What changed in FIX-118?`; `Approve FIX-118`; `What changed in PLAT-77?`; `Please revise PLAT-77`; `What changed in PLAT-77?`; `Approve PLAT-77`; `What upgrades can I buy?`.

## Predictions and visible outcomes

The first review query was issued while APP-101 was still working. Expected: status explaining that the change was not ready and what could be done meanwhile. Actual: "I can look into APP-101 or take it on when you ask. There is no ready ticket right now; check the review queue." A later review request correctly showed a passing gate, summary, tests and supported approval. The first response obscured the active assignment rather than explaining it.

The privacy review explicitly said the default was enabled and approval would ship a known defect. The deliberate approval therefore had an understandable predicted consequence. The current Forge tab returned "Approving APP-118. On it." Its context bar changed, but the named escaped defect, FIX-118 recovery and provider policy update appeared only on Anthill's transcript. Switching tabs exposed those consequences. This is confirmed feedback routing, not proof that the simulation failed.

The cache review offered distinct promises and resource costs. I predicted bypass would deliver fresh data, defer dashboard speed and earn 7 trust without a stale-data repair. The owning Forge transcript later confirmed this outcome. An explicit optional follow-up was named. This is useful scope differentiation worth preserving.

Approving PERF-204 from Anthill returned only "Approving PERF-204. On it." and an unrelated session unlock. The actual 7-trust shipment and reduced scope appeared on Forge. Approving FIX-118 from Forge likewise left its repair payoff on Anthill. Cross-tab actions work but force a tab hunt for the result.

The PLAT-77 warning described a skipped worker smoke test and legacy shim. The generic revision changed its gate from blocked to passed and visibly changed the tests and warning. However, the player's action was still the same review/revise/review/approve loop. Unlike the cache remedy, this encounter offered no competing scope decision. This is a structural observation, not a request to add a menu to every ticket.

At five shipments, "What upgrades can I buy?" returned "Tell me what you want to work on in your own words. The next ready ticket is API-88 ..." It did not answer the upgrade query or direct the player to Terminal. This is a confirmed conversation coverage failure in a central progression topic.

## Ticket candidates

1. Add truthful conversational handling for active-work and upgrade inquiries, keeping questions non-mutating. Verify the exact inputs above, before and after review readiness, with focused parser tests and rendered-browser checks. Include natural model controls only if their affirmative intent and inquiry behaviour are separately tested.
2. Show a concise result receipt in the invoking tab for cross-provider actions, while retaining the owning session's transcript and future background events. Verify ship reward, escaped finding and repair state without navigating away. Preserve session ownership and do not duplicate the global event log.
3. Compare repetitive generic review loops with one additional consequential scope encounter only after correctness fixes. The cache's visible trade-off is a positive control. Require a changed decision under matched resource states; reject a candidate that just adds text or commands.

## Limits

This was not a full route, a mobile check, population research or a baseline-versus-candidate preference test. The human enjoyment hypothesis remains unvalidated. The report establishes specific visible behaviours and expert taste hypotheses; deterministic policy findings and the fresh-context explorer report are separate evidence.
